Delete Your Hubnex AI Account & Data

Hubnex AI gives users full control over their data. You can permanently delete your account and all associated information directly from inside the app.

Have a Pro or Max Subscription? Cancel It First

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Google Play, so you must cancel them there, or you may continue to be charged:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app
  2. Tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions
  3. Select Hubnex AI and tap Cancel subscription

Refunds for past payments are handled under Google Play's refund policy.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open Hubnex AI
  2. Tap the menu (☰) icon on the top left
  3. Open Settings
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm the deletion

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

The following data is permanently removed:

  • Your account information (email, name, UID)
  • Authentication data
  • Chat history and AI messages
  • Usage statistics and token data
  • Any stored preferences

Purchase records kept by Google Play are not deleted, because Google holds them under its own policies.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be reversed.
