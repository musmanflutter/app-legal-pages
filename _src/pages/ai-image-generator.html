How to create an AI image from text

  1. Describe it

    Type what you want to see: the subject, the style, the colors and the mood.

  2. Generate

    Hubnex AI creates the image in your chat in a few seconds.

  3. Reply to change it

    Reply to the image with what to change, like "make it night time", to get a new version.

Edit AI images by replying

Don't start over when an image is almost right. Reply to it in the chat with one change, like "make the background a beach at sunset" or "add a red scarf", and Hubnex AI creates a new version that keeps the rest.

AI image prompt ideas to try

Good prompts name the subject, the style and the details. Not sure what to write? Ask any model in Hubnex AI to write the prompt for you, then generate it.

  • "A cozy reading corner by a rainy window, warm light, watercolor style"
  • "Minimal flat logo of a paper plane, blue and purple gradient, white background"
  • "YouTube thumbnail background, neon city street at night, no text"
  • "Cute cartoon cat astronaut floating in space, sticker style"
  • "Labeled diagram style illustration of a plant cell for a school poster"

Tips for better AI images

  • Name the style: photo, watercolor, 3D render, flat illustration, sticker or pencil sketch.
  • Add details: lighting, colors, camera angle and background all change the result.
  • Change one thing at a time: reply to the image with a single change, so you keep what you like.
