Web search vs normal AI chat

A normal AI chat answers from what the model learned during training, which is great for explanations, writing and brainstorming. An AI with internet search reads current pages first, so it's the right choice for anything that changes: news, prices, schedules, scores and new releases. In Hubnex AI you get both in the same chat, so you never have to switch apps to check something recent.

Tip: say what kind of source you trust, like "use official sites" or "only news from this week", to get more useful results.

Web search makes AI answers more current, but it doesn't make them perfect. Open the sources for anything important, especially health, money or legal topics. For research projects, combine web search with chat with PDF to read full papers, and see AI for students for study tips.
