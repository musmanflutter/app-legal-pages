How people use a free AI assistant every day

  • Quick answers: explain a topic, convert units, plan a trip or settle a debate.
  • Writing: draft an email, rewrite a message to sound friendlier or fix grammar. See the AI writing assistant.
  • Study: get a concept explained step by step or turn notes into flashcards. See AI for students.
  • Work: summarize a long PDF, prepare for a meeting or brainstorm ideas.
  • Second opinions: ask Claude, then ask Gemini the same thing, and compare.

Long chats stay useful too: pin important messages with Keep so the AI still remembers them after 1,000+ messages, and reply to a specific message so the AI knows exactly what you mean.
