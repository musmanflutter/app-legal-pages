Hubnex AI vs Perplexity: which AI app fits you?

Last updated November 17, 2026 · BisonTech Studio

Short answer

Perplexity is an AI search engine: you ask, it searches the web and answers with sources. Hubnex AI is an all-in-one AI assistant: you chat with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok or Qwen, and Hubnex AI even includes Perplexity's own Sonar model for search. Pick Perplexity's app for search-heavy research on any device. Pick Hubnex AI for everyday AI help, with Perplexity Sonar built in.

Which one to choose

Choose Hubnex AI if you

  • Want to chat, write and study, not only search
  • Want to pick between ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini yourself
  • Want AI images and PDF answers in the same app
  • Prefer a lower starting price for paid use

Choose Perplexity if you

  • Mostly use AI as a search engine
  • Want sources on every single answer by default
  • Need iPhone, web or desktop apps today

Search engine or assistant?

The easiest way to decide is to look at what you ask AI every day. If most of your questions are "what's the latest on…" or "find me sources about…", a search-first app like Perplexity fits that habit.

If you also write emails, plan your study week, summarize PDFs, create images and want a second opinion from another model, an assistant like Hubnex AI covers more of your day. Its web search still gives you current answers with source links when you need them.

Pricing compared

Both are free to start. Perplexity Pro is about $20 a month. Hubnex AI Pro is $10 a month, and Max is $20 a month with flagship models from ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. See Hubnex AI pricing and daily limits.
