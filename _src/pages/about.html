About Hubnex AI

Hubnex AI is an all-in-one AI chat assistant for Android, made by BisonTech Studio, an independent app studio in Pakistan.

Why we built it

The best AI models now come from different companies. Using them all meant installing several apps, paying for several subscriptions and copying the same question from one app to another.

Hubnex AI puts ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Qwen and Perplexity in one chat, with Auto to pick a model for you, and the tools you expect from a modern AI assistant: web search with sources, image generation, file uploads and reasoning.
