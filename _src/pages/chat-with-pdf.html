How to summarize a PDF with AI

  1. Attach the file

    Add a PDF, TXT, CSV, Markdown or JSON file, or up to 10 photos, to your message.

  2. Ask your question

    Ask for a summary, a list of key points, or an answer to a specific question.

  3. Keep asking

    Ask follow-ups in the same chat, or switch models for a second summary.

What to ask your PDF

The AI PDF summarizer works for study material, reports, contracts, research papers and manuals.

  • "Summarize this PDF in 5 bullet points."
  • "Turn these lecture slides into study notes with key terms."
  • "What does this contract say about cancellation?"
  • "Explain the method section of this paper in simple words."
  • "Make 10 quiz questions from this chapter."

Tips for better PDF answers

  • Be specific: "Summarize the results section for a beginner" works better than "summarize".
  • Pin the brief: use Keep to pin your instructions so the AI remembers them through a long chat.
  • Ask for quotes: ask the AI to quote the sentence its answer is based on, so you can find it in the file.
  • Try a second model: if a summary misses the point, switch models and ask again.
  • Double-check numbers: AI can make mistakes, so check important figures against the original file.

Files you can upload

Hubnex AI reads more than PDFs. Upload PDF, TXT, CSV, Markdown and JSON files (up to 20 MB), or up to 10 photos of printed pages, handwritten notes or screenshots in one message, and ask the AI to compare them. You can paste an image straight from your clipboard too, and paste code as text. Once you have a summary, keep going in the same chat: ask for flashcards, a simpler version, or a list of questions to bring to your next meeting or class.
