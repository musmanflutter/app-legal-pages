Hubnex AI vs ChatGPT app: which should you use?

Last updated November 17, 2026 · BisonTech Studio

Short answer

The ChatGPT app gives you OpenAI's models. Hubnex AI gives you ChatGPT models plus Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Qwen and Perplexity in one chat, with Auto choosing a model per prompt. Pick ChatGPT if you only want OpenAI or need iPhone, web or desktop. Pick Hubnex AI if you want several top AIs on Android without paying for several apps.

Which one to choose

Choose Hubnex AI if you

  • Want ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini without three subscriptions
  • Like comparing answers from different models
  • Want Auto to pick the right AI for each prompt
  • Have very long chats and want pinned memory with Keep

Choose ChatGPT if you

  • Only need OpenAI's models
  • Need an iPhone, web or desktop app today
  • Rely on features that only OpenAI's own app has

The real difference: one AI or many

ChatGPT is excellent, but it's one family of models. Claude is popular for writing and long documents, Gemini fits naturally if you live in Google's world, and DeepSeek, Grok, Qwen and Perplexity each have fans for their own reasons. Hubnex AI's idea is simple: you shouldn't need a separate app and subscription for each.

Pricing: one subscription or several

Both apps are free to start. The difference shows when you pay. A paid ChatGPT plan covers OpenAI's models, and Claude and Gemini each sell their own plan on top. Hubnex AI Pro ($10/month) and Max ($20/month) cover models from all seven providers in one subscription, within a published daily limit. See Hubnex AI pricing for what each plan includes.
