Why daily limits apply

Running state-of-the-art AI models like GPT-6.1 Sol or Claude Opus 5.5 is very expensive. "Unlimited" plans often hide throttling or cut access after a small threshold.

To stay reliable, protect the service from spam and never surprise you with extra charges, Hubnex AI charges a flat monthly fee and gives each plan a fixed daily budget.

How the intelligent model cascade works

Instead of cutting you off when your allowance runs low, paid plans split your daily budget across three quality stages:

  1. Strongest (50% of the daily budget): you start the day with the highest-end models your plan includes, like GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Opus 5.5 or Grok 4.7 on Max.
  2. Mid-tier (35%): after heavy use, your prompts move to balanced models like GPT-6.1 Terra or Claude Sonnet 5.5, so you can keep chatting.
  3. Failsafe (15%): near the end of the allowance, very efficient models like GPT-6 Luna or Claude Haiku 5.5 keep your chat going.

When the whole allowance is used, the app shows a limit message until it resets.

Limit reset and upgrade rules

  • 24-hour reset: daily limits reset automatically every 24 hours, based on your local time zone.
  • Instant upgrade reset: when you upgrade (Free to Pro, or Pro to Max), your usage resets to zero so you get the full allowance of your new plan straight away.
