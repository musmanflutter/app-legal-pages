## Market notes
- **US, UK, Canada, Australia:** all keywords validated. These are the main SEO markets.
- **Germany:** Germans search the English "ai app" (34) more than "ki app" (9). "free ai" (43) beats "ki kostenlos" (21). English pages work, so no German pages are needed yet.
- **UAE, Singapore:** small search volume, and many terms read zero in Trends. Low SEO priority; social media and ASO will do more there.
- **Caution:** almost every term dips in the last 3 months in every market (sharpest in DE and SG). That looks market-wide, so compare terms against each other, not against their own past.

## Removed (and why)
- **Roleplay or companion intent:** no filter, no restrictions, like character ai / chai / polybuzz, roleplay, unlimited messages. These users want uncensored companion bots, and the free tier has limits.
- **Wrong platform:** for pc / windows / mac / iphone / ios. The app is Android only.
- **Features you don't have:** video generation, photo editing, humanizer, AI detector, text-to-speech, interior design, app builder.
- **Developer or B2B intent:** chatbot development, for website, api, ui design, icon, logo, vscode, model selection framework.
- **Other brands' navigational searches:** chatgpt down, gemini app, deepseek/grok app, ai chatbot nova, chatnow.
- **Student promo searches:** ai for students free 1 year / discount / offer.
- **Promises you can't keep:** chatgpt alternative free unlimited, free ai chatbot no limit. The free plan has daily limits.
- **Off-topic noise:** air fryer, Path of Exile ("poe"), class 10 books, virtual assistant jobs.
