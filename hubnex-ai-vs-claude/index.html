Hubnex AI vs Claude app: which should you use?

Last updated November 17, 2026 · BisonTech Studio

Short answer

The Claude app gives you Anthropic's Claude models, with Claude-only features like Projects and Artifacts. Hubnex AI gives you Claude plus ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Qwen and Perplexity in one chat. Pick Claude if you only want Claude or need iPhone, web or desktop. Pick Hubnex AI if you like Claude but also want other AIs without paying for each.

Claude is great. One AI just isn't always enough.

Claude is popular for natural-sounding writing, long documents and code. But no single model wins every task. Interest in Claude is growing fast, and so are searches like "ChatGPT vs Claude", because people want to know which one to use for what.

In Hubnex AI you don't have to choose once. Write your draft with Claude, ask ChatGPT for a different take, check a recent fact with web search, and generate an image for your post, all in one conversation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Claude in Hubnex AI?

Yes. Claude models from Anthropic are one of the seven model families in Hubnex AI. You can pick Claude for any message or let Auto-Detect choose it when it fits.

Is Hubnex AI cheaper than Claude Pro?

Claude Pro costs about $20 a month in the US (checked November 2026) and covers Claude only. Hubnex AI Max is $20 a month and includes flagship models from Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini, within a daily limit. Check Anthropic's site for its current price.

Does Hubnex AI have every Claude feature?

No. Anthropic's own Claude app has features that are specific to it, such as Projects and Artifacts. Hubnex AI focuses on using Claude alongside other models, with its own tools like Keep, web search and image generation.

Is Hubnex AI on iPhone like Claude?

Not yet. Hubnex AI is Android only. The Claude app is available on Android, iPhone, web and desktop.

Use Claude next to ChatGPT and Gemini

Hubnex AI is free to start on Android. Ask Claude, then ask another AI the same question, in one chat.
