Frequently asked questions

Do these prompts work in ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini?

Yes. They're plain-language prompts that work in any AI chatbot. In Hubnex AI you can try the same prompt with several models in one chat and keep the best answer.

How do I write a good AI prompt?

Say who the answer is for, what you want, the format and the length. For example: "Explain this to a 15-year-old in 5 bullet points." Then ask follow-up questions to improve the answer.

Can I use these prompts for free?

Yes. Copy, edit and share them however you like.
