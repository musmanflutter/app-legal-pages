Why write with several AI models

Every AI has its own writing voice. Some sound more natural, some more formal, some more creative. Instead of settling for one, ask ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini for the same draft in one chat, keep the best parts, and ask for a final version.

For long projects, pin your brief, audience and tone with Keep so every new draft follows them. Speak instead of typing with voice input when ideas come faster than your thumbs.

How the AI email writer works

Ask for an email in plain words, like "decline the meeting politely and suggest next week". Hubnex AI writes it inside an interactive email template, where you can edit the subject, the recipients and the body before you export it. Want it shorter or warmer? Reply to the draft and say so, and the AI rewrites it.

Writing for school? See AI for students. Writing about current events? Turn on web search for up-to-date facts with sources.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free AI email writer?

Yes. Hubnex AI writes emails on the Free plan. It creates the email in an editable template where you can change the subject, recipient and body before you copy or send it.

Which AI is best for writing?

Many writers prefer one model's style over another's, and it depends on the task. In Hubnex AI you can ask ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini for the same draft and keep the version that sounds most like you.

Can the AI writing assistant fix my grammar?

Yes. Paste your text and ask it to fix grammar and spelling, make it shorter, or change the tone to friendly, formal or confident.

Can I use AI to write my essay?

AI is great for brainstorming, outlines and feedback on your own draft. Many schools don't allow AI-written essays to be handed in as your own work, so check your school's rules.
