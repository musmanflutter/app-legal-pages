Hubnex AI vs Poe: which multi-model AI app is best?

Last updated November 17, 2026 · BisonTech Studio

Short answer

Poe and Hubnex AI both put many AI models in one app. Poe has the biggest catalog, with hundreds of models and community bots, and charges in points. Hubnex AI keeps it simple: the seven AI families most people use, Auto-Detect to pick one, and plain daily limits. Pick Poe if you want to try everything. Pick Hubnex AI if you want the top models without counting points.

Which one to choose

Choose Hubnex AI if you

  • Want the major AI models without browsing hundreds of bots
  • Prefer a simple daily limit over points
  • Want Auto-Detect to choose a model for you
  • Have long chats and want pinned memory with Keep

Choose Poe if you

  • Want to try as many models and bots as possible
  • Want to build or use community bots
  • Need iPhone, web or desktop apps today

More models isn't always better

A huge catalog is great for exploring. For everyday work, most people end up using the same few models: ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, plus DeepSeek, Grok or Qwen for specific tasks. Hubnex AI is built around exactly those, with Auto-Detect to pick the right one when you're not sure.

Points can also be hard to predict, because a long answer from a top model can use far more than a short one from a small model. Hubnex AI uses a published daily limit and a cascade that keeps your chat going with lighter models instead of stopping it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Poe and Hubnex AI?

Both let you use many AI models in one app. Poe, made by Quora, offers a very large catalog of models and community bots, paid with points that each model uses at a different rate. Hubnex AI focuses on six major model families with Auto-Detect, plain daily limits and tools like Keep.

Does Hubnex AI use points like Poe?

No. Hubnex AI plans have a daily limit that resets every 24 hours. Paid plans use a model cascade that moves you to lighter models near the end of your allowance instead of cutting you off.

Which has more models, Poe or Hubnex AI?

Poe has far more models and bots. Hubnex AI includes the seven families most people use: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Qwen and Perplexity.

Is Hubnex AI on iPhone like Poe?

Not yet. Hubnex AI is Android only. Poe is available on Android, iPhone, web and desktop.

A simpler multi-model AI app

Hubnex AI is free to start on Android: seven AI families, one chat, and plain daily limits instead of points.
