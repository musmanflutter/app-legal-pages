What is Hubnex AI?

Hubnex AI is an all-in-one AI chat assistant for Android, made by BisonTech Studio. It's an AI app with multiple models: it brings the leading AI models into one app, so you can write, research, study, code and create images with whichever model fits the task, without paying for and switching between separate AI apps.

Auto mode reads your prompt and picks a suitable model. Real-time web search adds source links, Thinking Mode shows step-by-step thinking, and Keep lets the AI remember pinned messages even in very long chats.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an app that has all the AI models in one place?

Yes. Hubnex AI is an all-in-one AI app for Android that puts ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Qwen and Perplexity in one chat. You can switch models in the same conversation or let Auto choose one for each prompt.

What is the best all-in-one AI app?

The best one is the app that gives you the models you actually use in one place. Hubnex AI is built for that: an AI app with multiple models, web search, image generation and file uploads, free to start on Android.

Is Hubnex AI available on iPhone or PC?

Not yet. Hubnex AI is an Android app, available on Google Play for Android 7.0 and up.

Which AI models can I use in Hubnex AI?

Seven AI families: OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), DeepSeek, xAI (Grok), Alibaba (Qwen) and Perplexity (Sonar). For example GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Opus 5.5, Gemini 3.1 Pro and Grok 4.7. The Free plan includes a fast model from every family; Pro and Max add stronger and flagship models. See every model by plan.

What does Auto-Detect do?

Auto-Detect reads your prompt and sends it to a model that suits it, so you don't have to guess which AI is best for each question. You can always pick a model yourself instead.

Is Hubnex AI a good ChatGPT alternative?

If you want more than one AI, yes. Hubnex AI includes ChatGPT models and adds Claude, Gemini and others in the same app. Read the full Hubnex AI vs ChatGPT comparison.

Is Hubnex AI free?

Yes. The Free plan includes AI chat, web search, image generation and file uploads with efficient models from every provider, under a daily limit. Pro ($10/month) and Max ($20/month) raise the limit and unlock the strongest models. See plans and limits.

When does my daily limit reset?

Every 24 hours, based on your local time zone. Upgrading resets your usage to zero straight away.

Is my data safe in Hubnex AI?

Data is encrypted in transit, and you can delete single chats or your whole account at any time from the app. Read the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

Open the menu, go to Settings and tap Delete Account. Your chats, usage data and account are removed permanently. See account deletion.
