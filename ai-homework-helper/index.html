AI homework helper

AI homework helper: snap a picture, get it explained

Stuck on a question? Take a picture of your homework, upload it to Hubnex AI and get a clear, step-by-step explanation from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Free to start on Android.

How to get homework help from a photo

  1. Take a picture

    Photograph the question, or take a screenshot and upload it. Make sure the text is sharp.

  2. Ask for an explanation

    Ask "Explain how to solve this step by step", not just for the answer.

  3. Check and practice

    Ask a follow-up, or ask for a similar question to try on your own.

Built for learning, not just answers

Step-by-step reasoning

Reasoning mode shows the thinking before the final answer, which is ideal for math and science.

A second opinion

Not sure about an answer? Ask a different model in the same chat and compare.

Ask about one step

Reply to the exact step you don't understand, and the AI explains just that part.

Subjects the AI homework helper covers

  • Math: algebra, geometry, calculus and word problems, with the full working shown in Reasoning mode.
  • Science: physics formulas, chemistry equations and biology diagrams explained in simple words.
  • English: grammar questions, reading comprehension and feedback on your own sentences.
  • History and geography: causes, events and dates, with web search for sources you can cite.
  • Languages: translations with an explanation of the grammar behind them.
  • Computer science: code that won't run, with the bug found and explained.

Photos work best when the question is flat, well lit and fully in the frame. If the AI misreads a number, reply to its answer and type the correct value.

Use an AI homework helper the right way

An AI homework helper works best as a tutor. Ask it to explain the method, give you a hint or check your own working. Copying answers you don't understand won't help in an exam, and handing in AI-written work can break school rules.

A good habit: after the AI explains a problem, close the chat and try a similar one yourself. Then ask the AI to check your working. That's how the method sticks before the exam.

Frequently asked questions

Can AI help with homework from a picture?

Yes. Upload a photo or screenshot of the question in Hubnex AI and ask the AI to explain how to solve it. You can also paste an image straight from your clipboard.

Is the AI homework helper free?

Yes. Image upload and chat are included in the Free plan with a daily limit.

Which subjects can it help with?

Math, science, English, history, languages, coding and more. For math and logic, turn on Reasoning mode to see the full step-by-step working.

Will the AI always be right?

No. AI can make mistakes, especially with blurry photos or tricky problems. Check the steps, ask a second model to confirm, and use the explanation to learn the method.
