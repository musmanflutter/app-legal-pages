Why Daily Limits Apply

Running state-of-the-art AI models (like GPT-5.4 or Claude Opus) is extremely computationally expensive. Traditional "unlimited" plans often include hidden throttling or restrict access entirely after a small threshold.

To maintain high reliability, protect our systems from abusive spam, and ensure we never surprise you with extra charges, Hubnex AI charges a flat monthly fee and allocates a strict daily API token budget to each tier.

How the Intelligent Model Cascade Works

Instead of cutting off access completely when your allowance runs low, Hubnex AI paid plans use an Intelligent Model Cascade. Your daily allowance is split across three distinct quality stages:

  1. Stage 1: Strongest (50% of daily budget)
    You start your day with access to the highest-end flagship models (like GPT-5.4, Claude Opus, or Grok Reasoning depending on your plan).
  2. Stage 2: Mid-Tier (35% of daily budget)
    Once your heavy consumption exceeds the first 50% limit, the app automatically and seamlessly routes your queries to balanced mid-tier models (like GPT-5.1 or Gemini Pro) to extend your chat time.
  3. Stage 3: Failsafe (15% of daily budget)
    Near the end of your daily allowance, the system shifts to extremely efficient but highly responsive failsafe models (like GPT-4o Mini) to keep your chat active.

Once your daily allowance is completely exhausted, the app will show a limit message. Your allowance and usage limits reset automatically every 24 hours.

Limit Reset & Upgrade Rules

  • 24-Hour Reset: Your daily token limits and spend statistics are reset automatically every 24 hours based on your local timezone.
  • Instant Upgrade Reset: Whenever you upgrade your plan (e.g. from Free to Pro, or Pro to Max), your token consumption count is reset to 0 immediately, giving you a fresh full allowance for your new tier right away.
